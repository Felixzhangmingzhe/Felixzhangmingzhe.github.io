Hello! I am **Mingzhe (Felix) Zhang** (张明哲), a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Western University](https://www.uwo.ca/) in London, Ontario, Canada. I am jointly supervised by **Prof. Boyu Wang** in Computer Science and **Prof. Pingzhao Hu** in Biochemistry.

My current research is at the intersection of machine learning and biomedical applications. I am interested in developing reliable, data-driven methods for biomedical problems.

Selected Project
======

**[Respiratory Pattern Classification from ICU Chest Videos](https://github.com/Felixzhangmingzhe/video-based-respiratory-classifier)**

As an undergraduate research project at the University of Toronto, I studied respiratory-pattern classification using optical flow, frequency-domain features, and a multilayer perceptron.
